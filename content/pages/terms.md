By using this site you agree to these few rules.

## Downloads
- Mods, plugins and games are free unless their page says otherwise, and come **as they are**. Use them at your own risk and keep backups of your server.
- Some releases have their own license or rules on their page. Those apply too.
- Don't sell or re-upload Project Neutral releases as your own.

## Accounts
- Log in only with your own GitHub or Google account.
- Don't try to get into other accounts, the Admin page, or the bot.
- Accounts that break these rules can be removed.

## The site
- The site and its content can change or go offline at any time.
- Links to other sites (like Discord or GitHub) follow those sites' own rules.

## Contact
Questions go to the Project Neutral Discord, or open an issue at https://github.com/V13games/mods.

## Changes
If these terms change, the date at the top changes too.
