Project Neutral is a small site. This page says, in plain words, what happens to your information when you use it.

## What is kept
- **If you log in** with GitHub or Google: your name, profile picture and email address, and for GitHub your username. GitHub or Google sends these when you log in. The site never sees your password.
- **Your role** on the site: member, admin or owner.
- **Messages sent to Discord from the site** by the owner or admins: the message, the channel and who sent it.

Nothing else. If you never log in, nothing about you is kept.

## What it is used for
Only to run your account and the site. It isn't sold, shared for ads, or used to send you emails.

## Where it is kept
- Accounts are stored with **Supabase**, on servers in the EU (Ireland).
- The website itself is hosted by **GitHub Pages**.
- The Games tab loads from v13games.github.io, and the Discord numbers come from discord.com.

## Cookies
There are no tracking or advertising cookies. Your browser keeps a small note so you stay logged in, and it's removed when you log out.

## Deleting your account
Ask on the Project Neutral Discord, or open an issue at https://github.com/V13games/mods, and your account and everything linked to it will be deleted.

## Changes
If this page changes, the date at the top changes too.
